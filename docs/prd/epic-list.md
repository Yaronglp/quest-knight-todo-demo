# Epic List

## Epic 1: Foundation & Core Gameplay Loop
**Goal:** Establish project infrastructure and deliver the essential gamification experience - users can create urgent tasks that spawn dragons, complete them through animated battles, and gain XP/levels with full persistence.

**Value Delivered:** A working proof-of-concept that demonstrates the core value proposition. After Epic 1, stakeholders can experience the fundamental "task completion as adventure" mechanic and validate whether the gamification approach works. This epic is independently deployable and testable.

---

## Epic 2: Visual Polish, Enemy Variety & Demo Readiness  
**Goal:** Enhance gameplay with idle animations, non-urgent enemy types (goblins), and UI/UX polish to create a demo-ready showcase application.

**Value Delivered:** The complete MVP experience with visual richness and enemy variety based on task urgency. After Epic 2, the application is ready for public demos and presentations. This epic transforms the proof-of-concept into a polished product.
